Project Details

  • Location: Guwahati

Project Overview

This project required meticulous space planning, high-end decor, custom lighting, sound systems, and catering facilities. The design focuses on creating an elegant and functional space that reflects cultural aesthetics and provides a luxurious ambiance. The construction phase ensures quality and attention to detail, resulting in a venue that offers a memorable experience for wedding celebrations. The goal is to create a sophisticated and enchanting environment that meets the needs and expectations of clients, making their special day truly unforgettable.
